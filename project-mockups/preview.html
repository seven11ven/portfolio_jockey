Preview and "Edit in another app"

Previews are decrypted in memory. Editing makes a private, temporary readable copy, which is re-encrypted and wiped when you're done.

1. Preview (image)
Raster images open in memory and fit the available space. SVG files render as vectors on a transparent checkerboard; scripts and external references are not loaded. Image bytes and decoded images are cleared when the preview closes.

8. Edit in another app inside a Flatpak
A sandboxed app can't list your installed apps, so your desktop's own app chooser appears after this confirmation. Outside a Flatpak, scene 3's list is shown. Inside the Flatpak, the copy has to go in the memory folder Flatpak shares with your desktop, or the other app can't open it (found in testing, 2026-10-23), so the notice says so every time.
←

Kitchen Remodel

Edit measurements.txt in another app?

Your desktop will ask which app to open it with.

While you edit, a readable copy of this file is kept in memory where other apps on this device can read it. Your desktop needs that to let the other app open it. The copy is never written to disk, and it's wiped when you save or discard your changes. The other app may keep its own history of the file.
