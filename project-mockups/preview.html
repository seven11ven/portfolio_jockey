Preview and "Edit in another app"

Previews are decrypted in memory. Editing makes a private, temporary readable copy, which is re-encrypted and wiped when you're done.

1. Preview (image)
Images, PDF, text and Markdown render directly. Audio and video use the same screen with player controls (the playback approach is decided in build phase 6).
