4a. Move to Trash (the original)
The original goes to the system Trash (through the Trash portal inside a Flatpak), so it can be restored. The encrypted copy in the project is kept. Afterwards a notice says “contractor-quote.pdf: original moved to Trash”.

5. When the original can't be deleted
For example, the original was moved or deleted already, or on Android it was shared in from another app that didn't grant lasting access. The two options are greyed out, with the reason shown.
